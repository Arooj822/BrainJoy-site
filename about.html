About BrainJoy

BrainJoy is a chemistry and science blog for students who want the subject explained the way a good teacher would explain it — clearly, honestly, and without unnecessary jargon.

It's written by a Chemistry teacher with over nine years in the classroom, across British and American curricula, currently teaching in the UAE. BrainJoy exists to bring that same clarity outside the classroom: breaking down real chemistry news, exam strategies, and honest conversations about where a science education can actually take you — especially for students in Pakistan and across South Asia who don't always see themselves represented in global science media.

No stress, no gatekeeping — just science that sparks curiosity instead of dread.
