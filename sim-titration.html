pH Curve

What is an equivalence point?

The equivalence point is where moles of acid exactly equal moles of base. For a strong acid + strong base titration, this occurs at pH 7. The indicator changes color near this point to signal the endpoint.

Why does the pH jump so fast?

Near the equivalence point, the solution is a buffer that resists pH change — until it suddenly can't anymore. One extra drop of base causes a massive pH swing from ~3 to ~10. That's the steep part of the curve.
