Commonwealth Scholarships

Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (UK)

UK Master's PhD

Fully-funded master's and PhD study in the UK for students from developing Commonwealth countries, including Pakistan. Covers science and technology fields alongside development-focused subjects. Pakistani applicants apply via HEC, which nominates candidates to the Commission.

FundingFull tuition + ~£1,378–1,690/mo
WindowAnnual, via HEC portal (~Oct)
WhoPakistani/AJK nationals, 2:1 honours+

Chevening Scholarships

UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office

UK Master's

Fully-funded one-year UK master's for future leaders from over 160 countries, open to any subject including chemistry and materials science. Pakistan is consistently one of the largest recipient countries. Requires ~2 years' work experience and a 2-year return commitment.

FundingFull tuition, stipend, travel
WindowOpens ~Aug, closes ~early Oct (annual)

OWSD PhD Fellowships for Women Scientists

Organization for Women in Science for the Developing World

Global PhD Women only

Funds up to 4 years of PhD research (natural sciences, incl. chemistry) at a host institute in another country of the Global South, for women from science-and-technology-lagging countries. Note: OWSD does not run a call every single year — confirm the current status before planning around it.

FundingStipend + fees + travel, up to 4 yrs
WindowNot annual — check OWSD site for open calls
WhoWomen scientists, eligible developing countries
