❌ What Dalton Claimed

Atoms are indivisible, solid spheres — the smallest unit of matter. No subatomic particles exist.

✅ What Thomson Proved

Cathode rays are streams of negatively charged particles (electrons) — atoms ARE divisible!

🎯 Key Evidence That Disproved Dalton

  • Deflection toward positive plate → proves cathode rays carry negative charge
  • Same deflection regardless of cathode material → electrons exist in ALL atoms
  • Mass/charge ratio measured → particles are ~1/1836 mass of hydrogen atom
  • Conclusion: Atoms contain smaller subatomic parts → Dalton's model is wrong!
