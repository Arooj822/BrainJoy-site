What does pH actually measure?

pH measures the concentration of hydrogen ions (H⁺) in a solution. Lower pH means more H⁺ ions — more acidic. Higher pH means fewer H⁺ ions and more OH⁻ ions — more basic. Each whole number change represents a 10× difference in concentration.

Why does universal indicator change color?

Universal indicator is a mixture of dyes that change color at different pH values. In strong acid it's red, in strong base it's purple, and at neutral pH it's green. It's like a chemical rainbow that tells you the exact acidity.
